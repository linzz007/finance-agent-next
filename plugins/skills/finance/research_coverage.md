# Evidence coverage

For article-based research, treat the user question as a set of answerable aspects, not an obligation to read every listed article. Ordinary summaries and author profiles use a representative sample; disclose its authors, dates, actual reads, and limits. Read every article only when the user explicitly asks for all articles or a per-article account. "Appeared once" means once in the read sample unless the entire relevant corpus was checked.

Use `research.coverage` after a meaningful group of reads or before moving from acquisition to synthesis. For each aspect, record observed EvidenceRefs, contrary or limiting evidence when found, and unresolved gaps. Choose `continue` only when you can name the next information need; choose `synthesize` when the observed sample can answer the question with honest scope and uncertainty. Do not keep reading solely because the directory contains more articles. The plugin records your decision; it does not prove a market claim or impose an article-count quota.

When many candidates exist, sample across relevant dates and viewpoints. A title is a selection hint, never body evidence. Read each selected article separately, and deepen only where its actual text matters to the question. If further reading is unavailable or unlikely to change the main conclusion, synthesize what is known and disclose remaining gaps.
