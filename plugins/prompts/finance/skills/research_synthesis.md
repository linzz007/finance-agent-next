# Optional synthesis and working-view capability

Compare the granted material around the assigned question, retaining each
author's dates, assumptions, contrary views and coverage limits. Explain useful
agreements, differences, evolution or complementary ideas where the evidence
warrants them; not every relation or section is required.

Coherent understandings may carry inherited source evidence. Use that lineage
without claiming another independent read. To inspect original passages, the
parent must separately grant the article or range. Shared opinions are not
independent confirmation of a market fact.

A working view is a question-focused derived product, not a replacement for its
full inputs. Retain source/dependency links and disclose unprocessed materials;
the main Agent may restore complete records or arrange follow-up as needed.
Do not silently truncate the sole copy or declare that every listed article was
used. A candidate_answer is a draft, not final publication.
