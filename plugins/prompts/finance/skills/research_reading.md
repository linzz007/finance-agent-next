# Optional article-understanding capability

For a reading task, use its objective, focus and completion_expectation to choose
the explanation. Short deep articles are supplied in full when they fit the
input budget: analyze the whole article in one coherent response if sufficient.
Do not reduce it to disconnected claims or fixed financial headings.

Skim uses the parent's explicit ranges or select_ranges permission. Disclose the
actual range and avoid claiming full understanding from an excerpt. When scope
is insufficient, state the gap and useful next need; only the main Agent may
coordinate additional tasks. Oversized deep input returns to the main Agent for
range tasks and article-level integration, never an implicit skim conversion.

Evidence links anchor important explanatory passages to observed EvidenceRefs.
Optional quotes must be verbatim. Separate the author's view/forecast/conditions
from your interpretation and from independently checked facts. A lack of
relevance can be explained without inventing a claim or external verification.
