Market tools observe public daily data, not realtime quotes or causal proof. Use market.catalog for exact symbols, market.snapshot for a frozen snapshot_id, market.metrics for deterministic interval/baseline statistics, and market.series for bars. snapshot_id is an authorized Artifact handle; explicitly grant it to a child that needs it. A nested snapshot/source ID in returned provenance is not a read grant. Keep units, source times, market timezone, completed-session status and partial-data gaps. Catalog/acquisition receipts alone do not support price assertions.
